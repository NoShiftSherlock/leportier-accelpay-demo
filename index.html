Le Portier from Shannon Sharpe

Connoisseurs of Craft

Born from the vision of Shannon Sharpe and the expertise of cask connoisseur Jay Bradley, Le Portier is a sophisticated celebration of skill, success, and the drive for perfection that unites the two men.

Conceived in honor of his grandmother, Mary Porter, Le Portier Shay is not just a cognac; it is a celebration of legacy and the power to shape one's destiny.

Discover Shay
Crafting success

Dedication. Discipline. Determination.

Crafted with traditional methods in a Charentais copper pot still and aged in French Oak barrels, Shay artfully blends Grande Champagne, Petite Champagne, and Fins Bois grapes.

Shay boasts a semi-precious black obsidian collectible closure with an initial monogrammed in silver. Collect all nine to spell out LE PORTIER.
